# Product Mission

## Problem

Building companies involved in legal proceedings are hard to track. The NSW Online Registry only exposes ~3 weeks of data at a time, and the same company may appear under multiple trading names — making manual monitoring impractical.

## Target Users

Consumers researching builders before signing contracts. They need to know whether a builder has an active or historical history of court cases before committing.

## Solution

Automatically scrapes and indexes the NSW Online Registry on a continuous basis, preserving historical cases beyond the 3-week API window. Groups all trading names and aliases under a single builder entity, so a search for any known name returns the full combined case history.
